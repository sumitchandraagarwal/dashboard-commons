## Good logistics buy better family days {#krishna}

Krishna travels well. The failure mode is accumulated fatigue, formal indoor evenings and too many transitions—not movement itself.

<div class="family-grid">
  <article><span>01</span><h3>One anchor per day</h3><p>Make lunch the ambitious meal. Favor outdoor or early dinner and protect a low-demand day after every major move.</p></article>
  <article><span>02</span><h3>Doona + Deuter</h3><p>Bring both. On travel days the Deuter carries packing cubes while Krishna rides in the Doona; if he switches, the cubes move into the stroller.</p></article>
  <article><span>03</span><h3>Hands and backs</h3><p>One hand pushes Krishna, one may push the shared roller, and everything else lives on a back. No loose totes.</p></article>
  <article><span>04</span><h3>Sleep flexibility</h3><p>The stroller preserves a real nap option on longer days. A separate hotel bedroom is a luxury, not a trip requirement.</p></article>
</div>

<div class="monitor-grid">
